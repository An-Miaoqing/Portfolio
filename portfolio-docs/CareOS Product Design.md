# **I would make one design rule**

**Every chapter follows exactly the same rhythm.**

1. Business idea
2. Diagram
3. Interactive explanation
4. Real screenshots
5. Why it matters

That consistency is what makes great product sites feel effortless.

# **One idea I’d love to add**

Looking at all your documentation, I noticed you’re consistently thinking at **different abstraction levels**.

Business.

System.

Implementation.

So I’d make that an explicit feature.

A small toggle in the top corner:

```
● Business

○ System

○ Technical
```

The content changes without leaving the page.

For example, the same lifecycle becomes:

### **Business**

```
Customer

↓

Service

↓

Payment
```

### **System**

```
Booking

↓

Visit

↓

Assignment

↓

Invoice
```

### **Technical**

```
POST /api/bookings

↓

Booking Service

↓

Prisma

↓

PostgreSQL
```

It’s the same process, but tailored to different audiences.

I think that’s one of the most distinctive ideas for your portfolio because it reflects how a Business Systems Analyst thinks: translating between business, system, and technical perspectives.
